> **"Engineers talk about microvolts; Investors talk about ROI. I speak both."**

## 🎯 The Mission
Companies building complex machinery—from Green Hydrogen Electrolyzers to Autonomous Ag-Tech—often face a "Communication Gap." They have world-class engineering but struggle to explain the **financial value** of that engineering to investors.

**I bridge that gap.** I translate dense technical specifications into clear, punchy, and bankable narratives that help manufacturers secure capital and help investors mitigate risk.

---

## 🔬 Featured Case Study: Green Hydrogen (PEM) Electrolyzer
In this project, I took a standard, overly-dense technical manual for a Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) Electrolyzer and redesigned the communication strategy for a Venture Capital audience.

### 1. The "Before" (Engineering Speak)
> *"The Model-X PEM stack utilizes a PFSA ionomer membrane with an IrO2 catalyst... ensuring a degradation rate of less than 15 microvolts per hour."*
* **The Problem:** Accurate, but fails to explain why an investor should care. It’s a "physics lesson," not a business case.

### 2. The "After" (The Investor Translation)
> *"We’ve built a machine that doesn't know how to quit. Our titanium-gold shield extends the hardware life by 4 years, directly increasing the Net Present Value (NPV) of your energy plant."*
* **The Solution:** Focuses on **Asset Longevity** and **Cash Flow**.
